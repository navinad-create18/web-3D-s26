Digital Oven

Click and drag over the screen to rotate it!

Scroll or pinch your trackpad to zoom in and out!

Click the pink buttons to show each coil

Adjust the temperature using the slider

Press the power button to show a food

Press the change food button to change foods

Press the up and down arrows to move the door

In this artwork, I imagined and experimented with a futuristic oven that is safer to use. I tried to achieve
this goal through creating holographic-coils that turn on and off instantly to prevent any burns
from residual heat, and remote controls for setting temperature. I also added a button
to change food instantly, with the ingredients in the future being able to generate
out of thin air. The design and door mechanism remains the same as the ovens we use today,
since it helps with continuity and recognition of what its use is.
