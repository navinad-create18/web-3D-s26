Pear Party

Use your mouse to click and drag over the image to rotate it. Scroll with your mouse or pinch your trackpad to zoom in and out.

- Press "C" to change the pear color

- Press "L" to change the light color

- Press "1" to scale the pears up

- Press "2" to scale the pears down

Click the "Save Image" button to save your image!
